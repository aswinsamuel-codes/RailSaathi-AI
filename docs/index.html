How it works

Six data-science engines, one pipeline

01

Multimodal fusion

Text, Hinglish, OCR from photos, image tags and voice transcripts are combined into one word + character TF-IDF space.

02

Categorisation & routing

A calibrated linear SVM picks one of 13 categories and 7 departments. Complaints it isn't confident about go to a human desk.

03

Cost-sensitive urgency

Missing an emergency costs far more than a false alarm, so severity is chosen by minimum expected cost, not by highest probability.

04

Extraction

Regex extractors pull the train number, coach and PNR from text and OCR; EXIF GPS maps the photo to the nearest station.

05

Duplicate grouping

Reports from the same train, coach and time window are linked into a graph, and each connected group becomes one incident ticket.

06

Predictive maintenance

Poisson gradient boosting forecasts weekly complaints per train and flags rakes that are deteriorating.
